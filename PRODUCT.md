# PRODUCT.md

## Product

**Name:** GEO Pro
**Platform:** Web (Astro 5 SSR, bilingual VI/EN)
**Category:** B2B service landing page — AI visibility optimization

## Audience

Vietnamese business owners (SMEs), marketing managers, and agency decision-makers evaluating whether their brand appears in AI-generated answers (Google AI Overview, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity). They are digitally literate but not technical — they understand SEO basics but need education on AIO/GEO. They browse on both mobile and desktop, often between meetings.

## Positioning

GEO Pro helps businesses get found, cited, and chosen simultaneously across SEO, Google AI Overview, and AI platforms. Unlike traditional SEO agencies, GEO Pro addresses the three-layer visibility model (SEO + AIO + GEO) with a structured methodology and measurable outcomes. No other local service offers this integrated approach with transparent pricing and a free AI Visibility Assessment as entry point.

## Core value proposition

"Tối ưu để thương hiệu không chỉ được tìm thấy trên Google, mà còn được AI hiểu đúng, trích dẫn và chủ động đề xuất."
(Optimize so your brand is not only found on Google, but understood by AI, quoted, and proactively recommended.)

## Durable constraints

- **Bilingual:** Primary language is Vietnamese (/) with English (/en) mirror. All copy must exist in both.
- **Brand colors:** Navy (#1f3864), Coral (#d05c4e), Lime accent (#90e202), Success green (#2d7a5c), Night (#050505).
- **Typography:** Montserrat for headings, Inter for body. Both loaded via Google Fonts or system fallbacks.
- **Brand name:** Always "GEO PRO" (caps in logo/nav), "GEO Pro" in running text.
- **Content structure:** 12 sections in fixed order (Hero, Why Now, Problem, Solution, Approach, Capabilities, Process, Case Study, Pricing, Commitments, Contact, Footer).
- **CTA pattern:** Free AI Visibility Assessment as primary conversion action. No hard sell.
- **Data claims:** All statistics must include source attribution where available. Disclaimer required on case study results.
- **Contact info:** Sourced from environment variables, never hardcoded.
- **Deployment:** Must work on Vercel (serverless), Docker, and VPS. Node.js adapter.

## What we actually have

- Real data: Statistics from Google I/O 2026, Chegg traffic loss, AI Overview user counts.
- Real methodology: 5-step process (Audit, Foundation, Acceleration, Expansion, Measurement).
- Real pricing: 3 tiers (Starter, Growth recommended, Enterprise) with Vietnamese pricing.
- Case study: One anonymized cosmetics brand (Ho Chi Minh City) with real metrics (4x, 9x, top-3).
- No logo file, no hero imagery, no testimonial photos. Design must work without custom assets.
